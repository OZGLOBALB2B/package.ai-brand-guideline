Icon & Logo

Incorrect Logo Usage

To maintain a consistent brand identity, always use the approved logo files and avoid the following:

  • Do not stretch or distort the logo. Always maintain its original proportions.
  • Do not rotate the logo or change its orientation.
  • Do not alter the colors or use unapproved color combinations.
  • Do not rearrange or resize individual elements of the logo.
  • Do not change the typeface or recreate the wordmark.
  • Do not add effects such as shadows, gradients, outlines, or glows.
  • Do not place the logo on low-contrast backgrounds that reduce its visibility.
  • Do not use busy backgrounds that make the logo difficult to read.
  • Do not crop or obscure any part of the logo.
  • Do not place text or graphics within the clear space around the logo.
  • Do not use the standalone color version for everyday applications. Reserve it for special uses, such as swag, accessories, gifts, and exhibitions.
